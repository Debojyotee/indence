# Indence Website

A modern corporate website for **Indence**, focused on building the next generation of life sciences capabilities in the AI era.

## Overview

The website presents Indence's positioning, capabilities, approach, and contact information through a dark, premium visual design.

The current implementation focuses on the **Overview page**.
